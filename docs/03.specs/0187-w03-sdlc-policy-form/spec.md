# W03 SDLC Policy Form Technical Specification (Spec)

## Overview

This approved change contract is the first of the Policy packages of handoff W03-H29 and decision W03-D27 of the W03 stage of the external v14.7 execution package. Of the ten governance documents of the Policy role (W03-D27), nine (`.agents/governance/agent-execution.md`, `approval-and-safety.md`, `context-and-memory.md`, `document-authoring.md`, `document-lifecycle.md`, `formatting-and-linting.md`, `git.md`, `model-selection.md`, `quality.md`) already carry the common Policy form (SPEC-0175, W03-D06) on the `governance/rule` profile, while `.agents/governance/sdlc.md` is still the only member of the `governance/contract` profile, a Stage 99 local role with the six sections `Overview`, `Authority Boundary`, `Governance Context`, `Current Contract`, `Validation and Refresh` and `Related Documents` and no form. This package moves `sdlc.md` form-only to the Policy role by the W03-D06 mapping and removes the profile and template that only it used. On 2026-11-13 the user decided (recorded as W03-D41 to W03-D44 with this package's first commit) that `sdlc.md` folds into the `governance/rule` profile with its `path_pattern` extended (W03-D41), and that the rules of the policy documents take the ID form `<CODE>-R<NNN>` (W03-D42), inline IDs plus a required Rules table with a Level column (W03-D43) and a Status column for retired rules (W03-D44). W03-D42 to W03-D44 belong to the later Policy packages (rule IDs, the Rules table, the Level and Status columns and the check) and change nothing here; this package only records them. The package depends on SPEC-0182 being present in the base (`534ddae7` or later), which it is: the explained-none texts below follow its reason table, now owned by `.agents/governance/document-authoring.md` (lines 302 to 305) and enforced by the `BODY-SECTION-BARE` rule of `scripts/validate-markdown-profiles.py`, which refuses a bare statement in an authored Policy. The SPEC-0182 package left the tree; its frozen source is `574e8e601a6757efdaf258d9d7be424e0fd928e4:docs/03.specs/0182-w03-explained-empty-sections/spec.md` and the REQ-0003 Archived Lineage row summarizes it. The [Task](tasks/tsk-0001-w03-sdlc-policy-form.md), created in the same first commit, records execution and the [Plan](plan.md) orders the work.

## Contracts

- **Registry (W03-D41).** `governance/rule` (index 20) gains `sdlc` in its `path_pattern` alternation, which becomes `^\.agents/governance/(?:agent-execution|approval-and-safety|context-and-memory|document-authoring|document-lifecycle|formatting-and-linting|git|model-selection|quality|sdlc)\.md$`. The profile `governance/contract` and the template twin `common/template-governance-contract` are deleted with the template file; the profile IDs list of the `governance-operations` family in `lifecycle_domains` loses its `governance/contract` entry; the `common/template-governance-rule` definition lists `.agents/governance/sdlc.md` among its `consumer_paths`. The `policy` document role already covers `governance/rule`, so `REGISTRY_POLICY_ROLE` stays strict and unchanged. The `governance-document` local role keeps `governance/provider` and `governance/role`; W03-D20's governance-document group, "governance contract, provider, role", is not edited in place; W03-D41 states that it supersedes that group's contract member and the counts of W03-D06 and W03-D20, so the group reads "provider, role" and the nine local roles stay nine.
- **Form-only move (W03-D06).** `sdlc.md` takes the Policy form: Purpose (Objective, Authority), Scope (Applies To, Exclusions), Rules (Requirements, Enforcement), Exceptions (Exception Conditions, Approval and Review) and Ownership (Responsibilities, Change and Adoption). Mapping: `Overview` to Objective; `Authority Boundary` to Authority; `Governance Context` to Applies To; `Current Contract` to Requirements with its five H3 (`Ownership and placement`, `Proportional transitions`, `Change kinds`, `Cross-artifact conformance`, `Three axes`) as H4 and its numbered list and terminal-slot paragraph unchanged; `Validation and Refresh` to Enforcement. Frontmatter `type` becomes `"governance/rule"`; `version` goes from `"3.5.1"` to `"3.6.0"` (section growth, one meaning edit; document authoring policy) and `updated` to the commit date. No `Rules` table is added: the Policy template makes it optional now and the later packages decide it.
- **Related Documents (W03-D11).** The `Related Documents` heading is not kept. Each link moves inline by type: the normative links (Document Lifecycle Policy, Document Authoring Policy, Approval and Safety) become a bullet list at the end of Authority, as the nine policies already do; the navigation link (Common Agent Governance) goes to Applies To as the sentence `[Common Agent Governance](../README.md) is the common agent governance entry point.`; the evidence owners go to a closing sentence of Enforcement, `The checks are owned by the [Quality Policy](quality.md), the [Git Policy](git.md) and the Document Profile Registry (docs/99.templates/registry.json).` A link that is also inline in Requirements stays there as well, as in `quality.md`. These additions place links and carry no rule.
- **The one meaning edit.** The paragraph at `sdlc.md` lines 27 to 30 says "This document is the one `governance/contract` member because it owns an invariant that other owners consume rather than a procedure: each change kind sets a minimum record set, check set and authoring approval (the change-kind table under Current Contract)." It becomes "This document is a Policy on the `governance/rule` profile because it owns an invariant that other owners consume rather than a procedure: each change kind sets a minimum record set, check set and authoring approval (the change-kind table under Requirements)." Two phrases change: the claim to be the only member of a profile that no longer exists is replaced by the claim to be a Policy on `governance/rule`, and the section name the table sits under follows the rename. The invariant, the three things it sets and every rule stay as written. This is the only edit of meaning; the Task records the sentence before and after.
- **Explained none (SPEC-0182, W03-D31).** Three required H3 have no source text and take the reason table's sentences, with the document's own noun: `Exception Conditions` ("This policy defines no exception condition of its own; exceptions follow the approval and safety policy's operator approval route. If this policy needs its own, state it here."), `Approval and Review` ("This policy defines no exception approval or review step of its own; the approval and safety policy's operator approval route applies. If it needs its own, state it here.") and `Change and Adoption` ("This policy changes as a governed-document change under the work lifecycle and the document lifecycle policy, with no adoption step of its own; if it needs one, state it here."). Two more H3 are pointers rather than bare statements. `Exclusions` is not bare: Authority already says that provider and agent-roster authority is outside Stage 99, so its line is a pointer, "Provider and agent-roster authority is excluded and stated under Authority; if another subject is excluded, name it and its owner here.", which W03-D31 keeps because the target exists and says what it claims. `Responsibilities` points to the `Ownership and placement` table, which states the owner and placement rule of each artifact; it is a link to `#ownership-and-placement`, which exists. The Task lists the final texts for review, and semantic review judges sufficiency.
- **Anchor consumer.** The one anchor consumer of `sdlc.md` is `.agents/governance/document-authoring.md` line 275 (`sdlc.md#ownership-and-placement`). The heading text `Ownership and placement` is kept exactly and becomes an H4; the anchor depends on the heading text, not its level, so the link survives. No other file links an anchor in `sdlc.md`; links without an anchor (the glossary, the READMEs, the skills, the workflows) are unaffected.
- **Decision rows.** The package commit appends to the SPEC-0161 Task: W03-D41, "`sdlc.md` Policy fold (W03-D27; W03-D06; W03-D20): `.agents/governance/sdlc.md` folds into the `governance/rule` profile with its `path_pattern` extended and moves form-only to the Policy role; the `governance/contract` profile, its template twin `common/template-governance-contract` and the template `contract.template.md` are removed; the `lifecycle_domains` entry is updated; the test fixtures that name the profile are retargeted. This supersedes W03-D20's governance-contract member and the counts of W03-D06 (governance rules) and W03-D20 (governance-document group). User answer 2026-11-13." Owner W03, applied by SPEC-0187. W03-D42, "Rule IDs take the form `<CODE>-R<NNN>`", W03-D43, "Rules carry inline IDs and each Policy has a required Rules table with a Level column", and W03-D44, "The Rules table has a Status column for retired rules", each with user answer 2026-11-13 and owner "W03 with W05; packages 2 to 5". The user's exact words are copied at commit time. Row numbers are re-read at commit time, because D32 comes from SPEC-0184, D33 to D36 from SPEC-0186 and D37 to D40 from SPEC-0185; the W03-H29 state cell is refreshed, not rewritten.

## Interfaces & Data Structures

- Registry: the `governance/rule` `path_pattern`, the `governance-operations` `profile_ids` list and the `common/template-governance-rule` `consumer_paths`; the removed profile, twin and template.
- Tests: the maps in `tests/test_common_form_tree.py` (`governance/contract` role entry, twin table entry and twin source entry), the seed and fixtures of `tests/test_document_lifecycle_cumulative_history.py` and the profile of `tests/test_task_execution_contract.py` line 1044 follow the removal; the existing tests fail after the registry change and before the retargeting and pass after it, which is the proof for each.

## Edge Cases & Error Handling

- Every document in the workspace that links `sdlc.md` without an anchor keeps working; the single anchor consumer is checked by the strict link validator.
- `sdlc.md` is loaded by authors and agents to choose an owner; the one meaning edit and the added links must not change a rule, so the Task records a before and after comparison of every sentence by section.
- Historical references to `governance/contract` in `docs/90.references/`, `docs/98.archive/` and `sessions/` record what was true then and stay; the link validator does not follow a profile name.

## Failure Modes & Fallback / Human Escalation

If any sentence of `sdlc.md` needs a change beyond the one named edit, or a section's explained-none text would be untrue, the author stops the package, leaves it uncommitted and asks the user; no bare line is kept. If any document outside the scoped files gains a diagnostic, or a validator or suite outside the listed test lines fails, the change is wrong and is fixed before commit. If SPEC-0182 is absent from the base when this package is implemented, the author waits and asks the user rather than writing bare lines.
