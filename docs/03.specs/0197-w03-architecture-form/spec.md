# W03 Architecture Form Technical Specification (Spec)

## Overview

This approved change contract is the third phase-3 package of the W03 stage of the external v14.7 execution package, in the order W03-D49 fixes. It gives the Architecture role its COMMON-DOC-FORM-1 1.0.0 form in the Stage 99 registry, gives the architecture description template its TemplateDefinition (W03-H13) and moves the template and the four active architecture descriptions to the form (W03-H02, W03-D01). It also carries out the second step of W03-D55: the architecture descriptions become the one place where the requirement-to-architecture relation is written, every requirement member is cited there, the hand-written `Downstream owner` column leaves the four Requirements, and a derived coverage check takes the place of its link check. It applies W03-D03 (`Lifecycle Traceability` and `Archived Lineage` stay registered local H3 under Traceability), W03-D08 (table contracts) and W03-D52 (every other existing H3 becomes an H4 and keeps its anchor), recorded in the [SPEC-0161 Task](../0161-w00-v14-7-delta-intake/tasks/tsk-0001-w00-v14-7-delta-intake.md), and reuses the model of SPEC-0169 to SPEC-0195 (recovery in [REQ-0003 Archived Lineage](../../01.requirements/0003-workspace-agent-governance-platform.md#archived-lineage)). ADRs are the next package and are not touched. A read of `main` at `de64c58d` found four descriptions, all `active`, 123 KB together (AD-0004 10 KB, AD-0005 10 KB, AD-0006 62 KB, AD-0007 40 KB), each with the same seven H2 against the form's six, 23 H3 other than the two local ones, 20 tables and 85 `Lifecycle Traceability` rows; AD-0006 and AD-0007 end with `Archived Lineage` (42 and 11 rows). Of the 68 requirement members, the descriptions cite 58: AD-0006 does not cite REQ-0003-FR-0032 and AD-0007 does not cite nine members of REQ-0004. Of the 14 fragment links into the descriptions, four name an H2 anchor the move removes, all in the `arc42 coverage` table of AD-0007 itself; no script or other document names a removed H2 title. On 2026-11-14 the user decided the two choices this package raised (W03-D57): the common contract's `Constraints` table is registered and no existing table is renamed, and an uncited requirement member passes the coverage check only with an explained exclusion line in its Requirement. This package's commit records W03-D57 in the SPEC-0161 Task. The package merges after SPEC-0195 and SPEC-0196, in that order, and the definition it adds satisfies the full-record check of SPEC-0196. The [Task](tasks/tsk-0001-w03-architecture-form.md) records execution and the [Plan](plan.md) orders the work.

## Contracts

- **Architecture form.** `common_forms.forms` gains the Architecture form exactly as COMMON-DOC-FORM-1 1.0.0 defines it (`roles[1]`): `form_id` `architecture`, `title` `Architecture`, Overview (Purpose and Readers, System Boundary), Context and Constraints (Context, Constraints), Current Structure (Building Blocks, Runtime and Deployment), Interfaces (Contracts, Failure Boundaries), Decisions (Decision Links, Quality and Risks) and Traceability (Requirement Mapping, Currentness and Lineage), every section required and `link_only` false everywhere. Templates keep their H1.
- **Local extensions (W03-D03).** Two registered conditional local H3 under Traceability, after Currentness and Lineage: `Lifecycle Traceability` (`architecture-lifecycle-traceability`), whose verification is the body-contract check, and `Archived Lineage` (`architecture-archived-lineage`), whose verification is the retired-identity reservation. Each records its condition, owner, review (when the Architecture form version changes) and removal (when architecture lineage has another registered owner).
- **Profiles and binding.** `sdlc/architecture-description` and its template twin keep `document_role` `architecture` and carry empty H2 lists; heading lists are derived from the form. Their body contract stays as it is: section `Traceability`, table heading `Lifecycle Traceability`, the columns `Upstream requirement`, `Quality attribute or boundary` and `ADR / Spec`, its enforced statuses and its source and target profiles.
- **Role table (W03-D57).** COMMON-ROLE-FORMS names the Architecture table as architecture id, constraint and affected components. W03-D55 renames a role table to the common contract where that table exists; none of the 20 tables in the four descriptions is a constraints table (they are quality scenarios, components, authority planes, delivery stages, the C4 element table, source and deployment mappings, arc42 coverage, transfers, `Lifecycle Traceability` and `Archived Lineage`). The TemplateDefinition records `Constraints` in `architecture-context-and-constraints-constraints` with the keys `Architecture ID`, `Constraint` and `Affected Components` (all required), `minimum_rows` 0 and row identity `Architecture ID`, the template carries its header, and no document table is renamed, as SPEC-0175 did for the Policy `Rules` table; the `Architecture ID` cell is free text and no identifier grammar is added. No description gains the table in this package, and `Lifecycle Traceability` is not treated as the role table.
- **Other tables (W03-D08).** `Quality Scenarios` in `architecture-decisions-quality-and-risks` records the current keys `Quality attribute`, `Stimulus`, `Environment`, `Response` and `Measure` (all required), `minimum_rows` 0 and row identity `Quality attribute`; the 21 rows of AD-0004, AD-0005 and AD-0006 pass unchanged and AD-0007 keeps its list. `Lifecycle Traceability` in `architecture-lifecycle-traceability` keeps its three body-contract columns, `minimum_rows` 1 and row identity `Upstream requirement`.
- **Citing rows complete (W03-D55).** Every member of an approved Requirement is cited in the `Upstream requirement` column of its architecture description. At `de64c58d` that adds one row to AD-0006 (REQ-0003-FR-0032) and nine to AD-0007 (REQ-0004-FR-0005, FR-0006, FR-0007, FR-0009, FR-0011, FR-0012, FR-0013, FR-0014 and NFR-0003); the count is taken again after the rebase. Each new row's boundary cell restates what the same description already says about that member and names the heading it comes from (for the nine REQ-0004 members, the `Transferred requirement members` table of AD-0007, which names all nine), and its `ADR / Spec` cell links the ADR the description already names for it or holds an explicit exclusion with its reason. The Task holds the table of new rows with their sources, and the user reviews it before any document changes.
- **Downstream owner removed (W03-D55).** The body contract of `sdlc/requirement` and its twin keeps the columns `Requirement ID` and `Acceptance criterion`, with no link column and no target profiles. In the four Requirements and the requirement template the `Lifecycle Traceability` table loses its third column, 91 cells: 80 with a link, 6 of them with a note after it, and 11 exclusions. The 68 member rows hold 60 of the links; the 23 acceptance-only rows, whose first cell is `N/A —` and names no member, hold the other 20. The citing rows and the coverage check account only for the 55 member cells that are a single link. The Task therefore holds a disposition table of the other 36 cells: the 17 that are not a single link and the 20 links on acceptance-only rows, one cell being in both groups. Each row of that table names where the fact lives afterwards: a description row that already names it (at `de64c58d` each of the 20 acceptance criteria is named by an `N/A —` row of its description), an `Archived Lineage` row that already states it, the citing row's `ADR / Spec` cell to which it is added, or a line under the Requirement's Downstream Links. The user reviews the table before the column is removed, and no cell leaves without a row in it. The requirement ID and acceptance-criterion cells of all 91 rows are byte-identical before and after. The Downstream Links pointer line that SPEC-0195 added to REQ-0003 and REQ-0004 names the derived view and the coverage check instead of the column.
- **Coverage check.** `REQUIREMENT-COVERAGE` in the link validator reads each `approved` Requirement's member identifiers from the identifier column of its body-contract table and refuses a member that no architecture description or Spec, in a status its body contract enforces, cites in its own requirement identifier column. It replaces the target link check of the requirement profile, reads only registry-declared columns, and fails first in tests. The `requirement-basis` entry of `relationship_contract` records the citing columns as the only owners, the derived reverse view, this rule and its tests, and no reciprocal carrier.
- **Uncited members (W03-D57).** A member that no citing row names passes only when the Requirement states it under Traceability > Downstream Links in one line that starts with the member identifier and holds `N/A —` and a reason, the exclusion grammar the body contracts already use. The check finds that section through the registry, by the form section id `requirement-traceability-downstream-links`, and the validator holds no literal heading text for it. This keeps what `allow_explicit_exclusion` allowed in the removed column, so a Requirement can still be approved before its description exists, and the absence is written once by the only document that can state it. After this package no current member needs one. A line without a reason is refused, and the rule is not report-only.
- **Content changes.** These are the only changes to what the documents say: the new citing rows; the removed column with the relocated facts; the reworded pointer lines; the four links of the AD-0007 `arc42 coverage` table that name a removed H2 (`Boundaries & Non-goals`, `System Overview & Context`, `Infrastructure & Deployment`, `Quality Attributes`), which name the form H3 that now holds that content; a positional word a move makes false; and the pointer lines the heading map names.
- **Version level.** Removing required H2 titles from the descriptions and a body-contract column from the Requirements is an incompatible contract change, which the document authoring policy rates major; each of the four descriptions and each of the four Requirements takes one major raise. The Requirements stand at 1.2.1, 1.2.3, 3.4.3 and 1.4.1 on `de64c58d`; the major raise of SPEC-0195 makes them 2.0.0, 2.0.0, 4.0.0 and 2.0.0, and this package makes them 3.0.0, 3.0.0, 5.0.0 and 3.0.0, whatever patch or minor raise a package exit adds in between. The descriptions go from 1.1.2, 1.1.3, 2.5.0 and 1.8.8 to 2.0.0, 2.0.0, 3.0.0 and 2.0.0. Both templates keep `version` `0.1.0`, the starting value of a new document, because template history belongs to the registry contract version and Git.
- **TemplateDefinition (W03-H13).** `common/template-sdlc-architecture-description` carries purpose, responsibility, non-goals, policy references (`document-authoring.md`, `sdlc.md`), vocabulary reference (`.agents/knowledge/glossary.md`), applicability, every real consumer path for its profile (registry, schema, its template, the four descriptions, the Stage 02 and Stage 99 READMEs, the profile, link, lifecycle and registry validators and the tests), validation cases, migration and `tables`. It stores the eleven definition keys of SPEC-0196, with `contract_version` `COMMON-DOC-FORM-1/` followed by `common_forms.version`, and the 19-field record that `template_definition_record` derives for it passes `REGISTRY_TEMPLATE_RECORD` against the schema copy. The record of the requirement template, whose `Lifecycle Traceability` table and `requirement-basis` sentences change here, still passes.
- **Heading map, applied per description.** The old H2 headings other than Overview and Traceability are removed and their bodies move under the form H3 named below in their old order. A row that names a heading wins over the row for its old H2; a paragraph is never split; a list is split only by items the source itself labels.
  - Overview > Purpose and Readers: the `Overview` text with `Current architecture summary` as an H4 in place.
  - Overview > System Boundary: the `Boundaries & Non-goals` body, except an item the source labels `Consumes`.
  - Context and Constraints > Context: the `Consumes` item, and `Consumer and validation flow` as an H4. Context and Constraints > Constraints: `Agent architecture requirements` as an H4.
  - Current Structure > Building Blocks: the `System Overview & Context` body with `Authority planes`, `System context and container views`, `Current platform sources and operating owners` and `Delivery assurance architecture transferred from AD-0010` as H4. Current Structure > Runtime and Deployment: the `Infrastructure & Deployment` body with `Deployment mapping`, `Local delivery stages` and `Release and work-tracking boundaries` as H4.
  - Interfaces > Contracts: the `Data Architecture` body with `State, identity and evidence`, `Terminal disposition and historical lineage` and `Skill comparison records` as H4. Interfaces > Failure Boundaries: `Loop and checkpoint` as an H4.
  - Decisions > Decision Links: the list items after the `Lifecycle Traceability` table that name an ADR. Decisions > Quality and Risks: the `Quality Attributes` body with `Quality scenario architecture paths` and `Implementation owners and remaining boundaries` as H4.
  - Traceability > Requirement Mapping: the list item that names the Requirement, and `Transferred requirement members` as an H4. Traceability > Currentness and Lineage: the list items that name a Spec that left the tree or implementation evidence, and `Architecture responsibility transfer` and `arc42 coverage` as H4.
  - `Lifecycle Traceability` and `Archived Lineage` stay H3, in that order, after Currentness and Lineage. The `Archived Lineage` section of AD-0006 and AD-0007 is byte-identical before and after.
  - A form H3 that receives no text gets one pointer or explained-none line. The Task records each description's placements, added lines and corrected words.
- **Templates.** The architecture description template takes the form's H2 and H3 with its author prompts placed under the H3 they describe, the `Quality Scenarios`, `Constraints` and `Lifecycle Traceability` tables and its frontmatter. The requirement template's `Lifecycle Traceability` table loses its third column.
- **Policy text.** The document authoring policy adds the Architecture to the roles that use their registry forms; the Stage 99 README says so for the SDLC forms; the Stage 01 and Stage 02 READMEs say that an architecture description cites each requirement member and that a Requirement writes no downstream link column.

## Data Modeling & Storage Strategy

Registry data: one form with two local extensions, one TemplateDefinition with three tables, four profiles' lists or body contracts, one requirement table contract and one `relationship_contract` entry. Documents: four descriptions with about ten new rows, four Requirements with one column removed, and two templates. Code: one link validator rule and tests.

## Interfaces & Data Structures

- `scripts/validate-links-and-owners.py`: `REQUIREMENT-COVERAGE`, built on the body-contract row reader the validator already has; a body contract without a link column is valid by the registry rule that allows both link columns unset, and a test shows the link validator accepts it.
- Tests: an uncited member of an approved Requirement fails; a member cited by a description row or by a Spec row passes; a draft Requirement is not checked; a citing document in a status its body contract does not enforce does not count; the exclusion line of W03-D57 passes and a bare one fails; a Requirement that has no Downstream Links H3 and an uncited member fails with a diagnostic that names the member and the missing section; the old three-column requirement table fails the body-contract column check.
- `tests/test_common_form_tree.py`: the architecture profiles gain their form, the exact form count grows by one, `FORM_TEMPLATES` gains the description template, the per-form loop gets an Architecture row with its body section, tables and two local H3, the record tests of SPEC-0196 cover the new definition, and the test that names the templates still without a definition loses the description template; `tests/test_document_terminal_contract.py`: the pins of the description profile's H2 lists and of the requirement body contract are rewritten.

## Edge Cases & Error Handling

- The merge order is SPEC-0195, SPEC-0196, then this package. SPEC-0195 moves the four Requirements and adds the handoff this package closes; SPEC-0196 adds `contract_version` to every definition, the schema copy and `REGISTRY_TEMPLATE_RECORD`, and retires the heading-line table path, so every table here names a section id. The Requirement edits are made on the then-current files after a rebase on `main`, because every package exit appends a row to REQ-0003 `Archived Lineage`.
- SPEC-0193 adds `LINK-FRAGMENT`, so the four `arc42 coverage` links fail the link validator until they name a heading that exists; every other fragment link into the descriptions names an H3 that becomes an H4 with the same anchor.
- The read found nine positional words (`below`, `above`, `following`) outside `Archived Lineage` in AD-0006 and AD-0007; each is checked after the move and a false one is corrected to name the heading.
- A Downstream Links line that names an acceptance criterion is prose; the coverage check reads only a line that starts with a member identifier.
- AD-0007 states its quality attributes as a list, so its `Quality Scenarios` table has no row and the contract allows that.
- The member count is taken from the tree after the rebase; a member added by an earlier package gets its citing row in this package.
- These authored profiles are checked for empty sections, so each added line is real prose and never a bare statement (`BODY-SECTION-BARE`).

## Failure Modes & Fallback / Human Escalation

If a sentence cannot be placed without changing its meaning, if a new citing row has no source in its description, if a removed cell has no row in the disposition table or its fact has no place to live, if a requirement ID or acceptance-criterion cell differs after the change, or if the `Archived Lineage` bytes or reserved identity set of any document differ, the author stops and asks the user. If any document outside the named scope gains a diagnostic, the change is wrong and is fixed before commit.

## Verification Commands

- Before and after, record the four strict document validators over the tree; diagnostics after must equal those before.
- For AD-0006, AD-0007 and the four Requirements: the sha256 of the `Archived Lineage` section and the set returned by `lineage_retired_identities`, before and after. For each Requirement: the sha256 of its ordered requirement ID and acceptance-criterion cells, before and after.
- For each description: the sorted non-heading, non-blank lines before and after, whose only differences are the lines listed in the Task.
