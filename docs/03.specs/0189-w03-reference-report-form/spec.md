# W03 Reference Report Form Technical Specification (Spec)

## Overview

This approved change contract is the third of three packages that close handoff W03-H28 and decision W03-D26 of the W03 stage of the external v14.7 execution package. The `reference-report` role of W03-D20 has a role and a TemplateDefinition but no form. SPEC-0186 gave six simple roles their forms and SPEC-0188 gave the governance roles theirs; this package gives `reference-report` its form, so only `provider-native` stays without one (W03-D33). W03-D26 and DOC-01 ask for a local form with H2 and H3 titles, order and content, registering the H3 titles the documents use, and for the documents to move form-only. On 2026-11-13 the user decided how: the research H3 titles are registered as they stand (W03-D35) and the audit, research and data reports share one reference-report form whose H3 titles are conditional per kind and whose Sources section is link-only (W03-D36). Both decisions are recorded in the [SPEC-0161 Task](../0161-w00-v14-7-delta-intake/tasks/tsk-0001-w00-v14-7-delta-intake.md) by the commit of SPEC-0186, which lands first together with SPEC-0188. The package covers the profiles `reference/audit`, `reference/research` and `reference/data`, their three template twins and the three templates under `docs/99.templates/templates/references/`, which bind 17 authored files today (2 audit, 15 research including `0002-archive-retention-and-provenance/m0001`) and no data report. It reuses the model of SPEC-0169 to SPEC-0188 (recovery in [REQ-0003 Archived Lineage](../../01.requirements/0003-workspace-agent-governance-platform.md#archived-lineage)). The [Task](tasks/tsk-0001-w03-reference-report-form.md) records execution and the [Plan](plan.md) orders the work.

## Contracts

- **Form identifier.** The `FormId` literal in `scripts/document_contracts.py` and the `form_id` enum of `document-profile.schema.json` gain `reference-report` as the last identifier, after those SPEC-0186 and SPEC-0188 leave. `DocumentRole` and its schema enum already hold the role and do not change; the comment above `FormId` and the comment above `DocumentRole` are corrected to name `provider-native` as the only role of the nine without a form. `common_forms.contract_id` stays `COMMON-DOC-FORM-1`; the form says in its `extension_policy` that it is a Stage 99 local role under W03-D20, W03-D26, W03-D35 and W03-D36, not a COMMON-DOC-FORM-1 role. The identifier and form counts are read from the landed state of SPEC-0188 before the tests are edited.
- **Form.** `reference-report` has title Reference Report, `h1` `{{document_title}}`, the `language_rule` string the other local forms carry, its `extension_policy`, and for each section `content`, `required_content`, `prohibited_content`, `empty_policy`, `validation_ids` (`FORM-STRUCTURE` and `FORM-CONTENT-REVIEW`) and `link_only`. A section id is the form id and the slug of the H2 title, with the slug of the H3 title appended for an H3 (`reference-report-scope-method`). The eight H2 sections are all required, in this order: Overview, Reference Type, Authority Boundary, Scope, Definitions / Facts, Sources, Review and Freshness, Related Documents. Their `empty_policy` is `explain_none_or_not_applicable`. `link_only` is true for `Sources` (W03-D36) and `Related Documents` and false for the other six. The `required_content` of `Sources` asks for each source's link, its evidence grade and its ISO retrieval date, as the research template's author prompts do, so a link-only section still has to state them; semantic review reads that.
- **Registered H3 titles.** A read-only scan of the 17 documents and 3 templates on 2026-11-13 (fenced code and comments excluded) found the H3 titles below. Research has 60 distinct (H2, H3) pairs: 18 appear in two or more documents and 42 in one, and 3 of the 42 are template titles (`Findings`, `Limitations`, `Recommendations`); the audit documents hold 5 pairs, all matching the audit template, and the data template holds 5 and no data report exists. The form registers 31 conditional H3 sections and 39 `local_h3` extensions:
  - Conditional H3 sections, `requiredness` `conditional`, `empty_policy` `omit_when_condition_false`, each with its own condition naming the report kind or history that uses it: the 6 research template titles (`Research Questions`, `Method`, `Claim-Source Matrix`, `Findings`, `Limitations`, `Recommendations`), the 15 historical titles that two or more research documents share (the 18 shared pairs less the three template titles `Research Questions`, `Method` and `Claim-Source Matrix`), the 5 audit template titles (`Criteria and Inspected Surface`, `Observed Evidence`, `Findings and Gaps`, `Remediation`, `Verification State`) and the 5 data template titles (`Schema`, `Repository Facts`, `Provenance`, `Generation and Refresh`, `Staleness Boundaries`). By H2: Overview 1, Reference Type 1, Authority Boundary 1, Scope 4, Definitions / Facts 11, Sources 4, Review and Freshness 9, Related Documents 0. Their order under each H2 is the one that satisfies every document and template (the scan found no order conflict); the form tree check enforces order among conditional H3 only.
  - Extensions, `kind` `local_h3`: the 39 one-document research titles that are not a template title under their own H2 (33 under `Definitions / Facts`, 3 under `Review and Freshness` and 3 under `Sources`). The three under `Sources` are the `Findings`, `Limitations` and `Recommendations` of `0002-archive-retention-and-provenance/m0001`, registered as `Sources` children (W03-D35) while the same titles stay conditional children of `Definitions / Facts` and `Review and Freshness`. Dated titles that only one document holds stay as `local_h3` with their exact text, em dash and date included. Each extension has `id`, `condition`, `owner` `platform`, `verification` `the form tree check`, `review`, `removal`, `parent_id` and `title`. The shared dated titles are part of the 15 conditional ones and keep their exact text too.
- **Profiles.** The six profiles (`reference/audit`, `reference/research`, `reference/data` and `common/template-reference-audit`, `common/template-reference-research`, `common/template-reference-data`) get empty `sections.required`, `optional` and `forbidden` lists, because `REGISTRY_FORM_LEGACY_LISTS` refuses H2 lists on a profile whose role has a form; the headings then come from the form. ## Core Design

[AD-0006](../../02.architecture/descriptions/0006-workspace-agent-governance-platform.md) owns the governance view; no component changes. The registry stays the single machine owner of document form and template definition ([ADR-0033](../../02.architecture/decisions/0033-common-document-contract-v9.md) as amended by [ADR-0059](../../02.architecture/decisions/0059-registry-common-form-model.md)); no ADR changes. One form serves three report kinds because the H2 set is the same for all three and the kinds differ only in which H3 titles apply, which a conditional section states; `Sources` is link-only because the three kinds list sources, and a section with H3 children is judged on its H3 children rather than on its own body.

## Edge Cases & Error Handling

- A conditional H3 and an extension may share a title under different H2 sections (`Findings`, `Limitations`, `Recommendations`); the registry check refuses a duplicate only under one parent, so the two homes are valid and the form tree check reads each under its own H2.
- Order of `local_h3` titles is not checked; only conditional H3 titles are ordered, so a later document may add a registered title only in the registered order.
- A section with H3 children is not judged empty or bare on its own body; a report that uses no H3 under an H2 must still hold authored prose there, and with `Sources` link-only a list of links counts as content.
- A dated title that one document holds is a `local_h3` record with that document as its only consumer; if the document is superseded, the record is retired in its own change, not here.
- No data report exists; the five data H3 titles are exercised by the template and by synthetic tests.
- If SPEC-0188 lands with a different identifier count, the tests in this package use the landed count; none is hard-coded from the pre-approval inventory.

## Failure Modes & Fallback / Human Escalation

Documents are expected to pass unchanged, so this package edits none of them. If any document or template of the role gains a diagnostic from the strict validators, the form is wrong and is fixed before commit, never the document; the author does not edit a governed document to satisfy a form, and never touches a published member. If a form cannot be written without a heading, sentence or table change in a document, the author stops and asks the user. If a published member would be staged, the author unstages it and stops. If `scripts/validate-document-contract-registry.py` or a script outside this list stops passing, the change is wrong and is reverted to the last passing step. After two failed attempts with no new information the author stops and reports a repository defect.
